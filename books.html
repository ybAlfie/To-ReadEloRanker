Add Many at Once

One book per line, as Title | Author | Cover image URL. Only the title is needed. Tabs work as separators too, so a spreadsheet column can be pasted straight in. Lines starting with # are ignored.

Royal Road and other web fiction

Royal Road has no public API and does not let other sites read its pages from your browser, so titles, authors and covers cannot be fetched automatically. Pasting a fiction link on its own line still creates the book, taking the title from the web address and keeping the link so you can click through. To get the cover, right click the cover image on Royal Road, copy the image address, and put it as the third field on that book's line.
