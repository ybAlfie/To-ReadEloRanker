About This Website

Description

This website helps you find which books from your "Want to Read" list you'd like to read next. Using a series of one-on-one matchups, you compare two books at a time, choosing the one you’d rather read. The website uses an ELO rating system (similar to Chess) to rank all your books, helping you decide what to read without having to compare each book individually. It works with a library export from either Goodreads or StoryGraph.

How to Use the Website

  1. Start at the Upload Page: On the Upload page, you can upload your Goodreads or StoryGraph Library Export CSV (or a previous rankings CSV if you have one). This will import your "Want to Read" list. Both go in the same box, the format is detected automatically.
    • Click here to export your library from Goodreads or go to "My Books", then click on "Import and export" under Tools.
    • Click here to export your library from StoryGraph or go to your profile, then click on "Manage Account" and scroll down to "Export StoryGraph Library".
  2. Go to the Ranking Page: After uploading, go to the Rank Books page. Here, you’ll be presented with two books at a time. Choose which one you'd prefer to read by clicking the book or pressing the corresponding arrow key.
  3. Keep Ranking: The system will continue to present matchups. Each choice updates the ELO ratings for the books, refining the rankings with each decision.
  4. View the Leaderboard: When you’ve made enough comparisons, visit the Leaderboard to see how your books stack up. You can also export your current rankings for future sessions.
  5. Merge Updated Files: If you’ve made changes to your Goodreads or StoryGraph "Want to Read" list since your last session you can upload both the new Library Export and your old rankings CSV together on the Upload page. The system will automatically update by adding new books, archiving titles no longer in your want to read list (they keep their ELO score, they just stop appearing in matchups), and preserving your previous ELO rankings. Importing one library never archives books from the other.
  6. Add Your Own Books: On the My Books page you can add books that are not in your Goodreads or StoryGraph library, including a cover image, and write notes on any book. Books you add by hand are never archived by a library import.

FAQ

Adding Books by Hand and Writing Notes:

The My Books page covers everything that does not come from a library export:

  • Add a Book:
    Fill in a title, and optionally an author, an ISBN, a link and some notes. Give an ISBN and the cover is looked up for you. Otherwise paste a cover image URL, or upload an image from your device, which is scaled down before it is saved.
  • Add Many at Once:
    Paste a list with one book per line, as Title | Author | Cover image URL. Only the title is needed. Tabs work as separators too, so a column copied from a spreadsheet can be pasted straight in. Lines starting with # are ignored.
  • Notes:
    Every book has a notes box on the My Books page. Notes save when you click away from the box, and they are kept in your rankings CSV. A later library import will never overwrite a note you wrote.
  • Archive or Delete:
    Archiving keeps a book and its ELO score but stops it appearing in matchups, and it can be restored at any time. Deleting removes it permanently.

Royal Road and Other Web Fiction:

Royal Road has no public API, and it does not let other websites read its pages from your browser, so this site cannot fetch a want to read list from it automatically. You can still add web fiction quickly:

  • Paste the Links:
    On the My Books page, paste Royal Road fiction links into the "Add Many at Once" box, one per line. Each becomes a book with its title taken from the web address, and the link is kept so you can click through to it later.
  • Add the Covers:
    To get a cover, right click the cover image on Royal Road, copy the image address, and put it as the third field on that book's line, after the title and the author.

Dark Mode and Small Screens:

  • Dark Mode:
    The button at the end of the navigation bar switches between the light and dark themes, and your choice is remembered on that device. Until you pick one, the site follows whatever your operating system is set to.
  • Phones and Tablets:
    The layout adapts to the width of your screen. On a narrow screen the two books in a matchup stack one above the other, the win and skip buttons become the main way to choose, and the leaderboard table scrolls sideways on its own rather than stretching the page.

Data Storage and Privacy:

Data is saved locally in your browser’s LocalStorage. This means:

  • Local-Only Storage:
    No external servers are involved. All the rankings and book data you generate stay on your own device, inside your browser’s storage. When you revisit the site using the same browser and the same device, your previously ranked books and ELO scores should still be available without needing to re-upload your files.
  • Privacy:
    Because no data is sent online, privacy is maintained. Others cannot access your rankings unless they use your device and browser.
  • Portability and Backup:
    If you want to take your rankings to another device or browser, you can export them as a CSV file. Without exporting, the data remains tied to the specific browser and device you’re using.
  • Space for Uploaded Covers:
    Browsers give a website only a few megabytes of local storage. Cover images you upload are scaled down before they are saved, but they are still far larger than the text of a book record, so a great many of them can fill the space. The My Books page shows how much is in use. If storage does run out you will be told rather than losing work silently, and pasting a cover image URL instead of uploading a file uses almost none.
  • Data Loss Considerations:
    LocalStorage can be cleared if you reset your browser’s data or run the site in a private/incognito window. Additionally, browser updates or certain privacy settings may occasionally wipe stored data. To avoid losing progress, make sure to periodically export your rankings as a CSV file.
